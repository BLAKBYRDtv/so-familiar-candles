    Good to know

    How long does my order take?

    Most orders ship in 7 to 10 days. Each candle is poured after you order and cures before it ships, so the scent is strong when you light it. Ordering for a holiday or event? Add your date and we’ll plan around it.

    What are the candles made of?

    Natural soy wax, cotton wicks and fragrance oils, poured into frosted glass, matte black glass or amber apothecary jars you can reuse.

    Can you make memorial candles?

    Yes. Use “Make it yours” to add a name and dates, or mention a photo in your notes and we’ll reach out about a photo label. Many families order several for a service or repast.

    Do you do bulk orders for churches, teams and events?

    Yes. Holiday gifts, church anniversaries, team parties, weddings and showers. Put the quantity and date in your notes and we’ll send a quote.

    How do I get the best burn?

    On the first light, let the wax melt all the way to the edge, usually 2 to 3 hours. Trim the wick to a quarter inch before every burn after that.
